# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

MadMusic is a small, private project. Everyone with access is here to build
something good, and that only works if working together is pleasant.

**Expected:**

- Be direct about code and considerate about people. Review the change, not the
  person who wrote it.
- Assume competence and good faith. Ask what someone was solving before
  concluding they were wrong.
- Give reasons. "This will break on iOS because X" is useful; "this is wrong"
  is not.
- Accept review as normal engineering, not as criticism.
- Say when you are unsure, blocked, or out of depth. That is information the
  project needs, not a weakness.

**Not accepted:**

- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning comments.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private, including unwelcome attention after
  being asked to stop.
- Discrimination or derogatory remarks concerning age, disability, ethnicity,
  gender identity or expression, nationality, personal appearance, race,
  religion, or sexual identity or orientation.
- Publishing anyone's private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption, bad-faith argument, or deliberately wasting others'
  time.

## Scope

This applies everywhere the project happens: the repository, issues, pull
requests, commit messages, code comments, and any chat or call where MadMusic is
being discussed. It also applies when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report a concern privately to the repository owner
([@MadBlast0](https://github.com/MadBlast0)). Reports are handled discreetly.

Responses are proportionate and escalate as needed: a private word, a formal
warning, temporary loss of access, or permanent removal from the project.

Maintainers who do not uphold this standard are subject to it too.
